About this tool

Graphify turns CSV or TSV data into an interactive chart in seconds. Paste data or drop a file, pick the rows you want to plot, and get a clean visualization you can read at a glance. Your data never leaves your browser — parsing and charting run entirely on your machine, so it's safe to plot sales numbers, analytics exports, and other private datasets.

How to use

  • Paste CSV/TSV text, or drop a .csv / .tsv file anywhere on the page.
  • The parser detects the delimiter, header row, and date columns automatically.
  • Select the series rows you want on the chart.
  • Switch chart types to find the clearest view of your data.

FAQ

Is my data uploaded to a server? No. Files are read and charted locally in your browser; nothing is transmitted.

What formats are supported? Comma-separated (CSV) and tab-separated (TSV) text, whether pasted directly or dropped as a file. Exports from Excel, Google Sheets, and most databases work out of the box.

My spreadsheet is in Excel — how do I chart it? Either copy the cells and paste them here (spreadsheets copy as TSV), or save the sheet as CSV and drop the file onto the page.
