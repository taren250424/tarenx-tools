About this tool

This free online calculator handles the everyday arithmetic — add, subtract, multiply, divide, percentages, and sign changes — and you can drive all of it from the keyboard instead of hunting for buttons with the mouse. Nothing is uploaded: every calculation runs locally in your browser, and the history is kept in the page only, so it disappears when you close the tab.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • 0–9 — enter digits.
  • . — decimal point.
  • + - * / — the four operators; x also multiplies.
  • Enter or = — calculate. Pressing it again repeats the last operation.
  • Backspace — delete the last digit.
  • Esc or c — clear everything.
  • % — percent.
  • n — flip the sign (±).

How to use

  • Display — the small line above shows the calculation in progress; the large line is the current number.
  • Percent — after + or - it takes a percentage of the first number, so 200 + 10 % gives 220. Otherwise it simply divides by 100.
  • History — every result is listed on the right; click one to bring that number back into the display.
  • Theme — the toggle in the header switches between light and dark, and the choice is remembered on this device.

FAQ

Are my calculations sent to a server? No. Everything is computed in your browser and nothing leaves your machine.

Why does a long result get rounded? Results are rounded to 12 significant digits. Computers store decimals in binary, so without that rounding 0.1 + 0.2 would show as 0.30000000000000004 instead of 0.3.

Is there a scientific mode? Not here — this is a plain four-function calculator, kept small and fast on purpose.
