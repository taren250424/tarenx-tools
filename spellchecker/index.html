About this tool

A spell checker for Korean and English that runs as you type. Your text is never uploaded: every check runs locally in your browser, so drafts, private notes, and unpublished writing stay on your machine. Type on the left and the corrected text builds itself on the right, with every change tinted in the color of its kind — spelling, spacing, loanword, punctuation — and counted in the legend. Click a tinted word to see why it changed, pick a different replacement, or keep the original. Copy result takes the corrected text; Apply writes it back into your own.

How the two languages are checked

English uses a Hunspell dictionary — the same dictionary format LibreOffice and Firefox use. A word is looked up, and if it is missing, close spellings are ranked by edit distance. That is the standard approach and it works well, because English words have few forms.

Korean cannot work that way. Korean is agglutinative: one 어절 is a stem plus however many endings the sentence needs, so the surface forms of a single verb cannot be listed in a dictionary. This tool therefore checks Korean with rules instead — the spelling, spacing, and loanword patterns that account for most real mistakes.

What it catches in Korean

  • 혼동하기 쉬운 표기 — 되/돼, 안/않, 왠/웬, 이에요/예요, -율/-률.
  • 자주 틀리는 맞춤법 — 어의없다, 오랫만에, 몇일, 역활, 설레임 등.
  • 외래어 표기법 — 메세지, 컨텐츠, 악세사리, 쥬스 등 100여 항목.
  • 사이시옷 — 최대값, 등교길, 갯수, 촛점 등.
  • 띄어쓰기 — 관형형 어미 뒤의 의존 명사(할수 → 할 수, 볼때 → 볼 때)와 앞말에서 떨어진 조사.
  • 부사의 '이/히' — 깨끗히, 일일히, 솔직이 등.

What it does not catch

Being clear about the limits is more useful than overselling: this checker aims for no false alarms rather than full coverage.

  • General Korean spacing. Splitting an arbitrary run of syllables correctly needs a statistical model. Only the bound noun and particle patterns above are handled.
  • Unknown Korean words. Without a morphological analyzer, a typo that happens to miss every rule passes silently.
  • Context. Neither language is checked for grammar, so real words used wrongly — 데/대, 로서/로써, English their for there — are not flagged.

FAQ

Is my text uploaded to a server? No. Checking runs in a Web Worker inside your browser. Nothing leaves your machine.

Why does the first English check take a moment? The Hunspell dictionary is about 550 KB and is downloaded the first time English text is checked, so Korean-only visitors never pay for it.

Why does one suggestion sometimes offer two options? Because only you know the word you meant. After a syllable with no final consonant, both 예요 and 이예요 are possible depending on where the noun ends, so the tool offers both instead of guessing.
